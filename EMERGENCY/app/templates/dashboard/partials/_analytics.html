    Figures cover incidents reported in the selected range, archived ones included. Pending = New or Acknowledged; In Progress = Dispatched, En Route or On Scene. Resolution rate = resolved ÷ total reported. Resolution time = report to resolution, resolved incidents only. Response time = report to first On Scene update. Critical is priority P1 and is counted inside the statuses, not added to them. Changes compare with the equally long period just before. Times are Philippine Time. High-risk areas are an analysis of past reports to support preparedness; they do not declare a place dangerous, and nothing is dispatched or moved automatically.
